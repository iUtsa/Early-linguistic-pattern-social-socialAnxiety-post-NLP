## Verdict

**I would not submit the present manuscript to a Q2, Q3, or Q4 journal. As a reviewer I would recommend rejection in its current form, with encouragement to undertake a new, narrower study.** The primary problems are validity and traceability, not insufficiently high F1 or a lack of deep learning. Lower-quartile journals still require genuine observations, valid labels, and defensible evaluation.

There is a viable project here: an interpretable, reproducible study of language associated with anxiety-related communities or self-reported anxiety. The present evidence does not establish diagnosis, clinical screening utility, clinical onset, anxiety-specific clinical validation, or psychological causation. These claims need to be removed or supported by a substantially different dataset and study design. A simple model can support a paper, including a negative-result paper, if it answers a specific question with credible controls. A conventional sentiment/pronoun classifier plus a web demo is insufficient novelty by itself.

## The study I would conduct

Working title: **“Interpretable linguistic signals in anxiety-related Reddit language: sensitivity to topic, author, and time.”** If a genuinely user-labelled dataset becomes available, change the title to identify self-reported anxiety rather than community membership. Do not retain “early detection” unless prospective or clearly pre-event labels are available.

The contribution should be a controlled answer to three questions:

1. How much of the 13-feature performance survives comparison with lexical, length-only, and pronoun-only baselines on identical held-out users?
2. How much of the apparent signal is explained by source community, writing genre, activity, text length, and explicit disorder vocabulary?
3. Which associations and predictive tradeoffs remain stable across time or a second dataset with a compatible outcome?

The novelty is evidence about robustness and confounding. It is not “we used interpretable logistic regression,” author-disjoint splitting alone, or minimal damage after masking a few words. A failure under topic matching can become a useful finding if documented honestly and connected to the literature.

### Data route and stop rules

The author identified [Rani et al. 2024](https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3417/14/4/1547) as the main source, and its methods now have been read. It describes 1,494,019 original posts from January 2019–August 2022 across five mental-health communities, with annual/monthly files and observed authors in raw Part A. That schema matches the supplied 2022 examples. Part B contains 800 manually annotated posts in four proposed root-cause categories, not anxiety diagnoses. This does not establish the exact anxiety/control sample, version, exclusions or permissions used by the preprint; general-interest controls come from other sources. The archived README's 2018–2019 dates require reconciliation.

The source paper reports Victoria University ethics approval HRE23-005 on 29 May 2023 and links its public Kaggle collection through `https://rb.gy/ewtjy`. Its approval is evidence about the original study, not automatic approval/exemption for the present secondary project. The Kaggle short link has now been resolved: [RMHD version 1](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/entenam/reddit-mental-health-dataset), publisher-stated CC0. All 15 authored uploaded filenames/byte lengths correspond to that release. The complete archive and all 15 supplied authored files are now SHA-256 verified; the author reports a current institutional determination exists, with its exact details awaiting documentation; see the [data card](DATA_CARD.md).

If real control author identifiers are recoverable under the data-use rules, build observed-user cohorts and audit them. If they are irretrievably absent, **abandon user-level early detection and unseen-person claims on this source**. A narrower post-source language comparison remains possible, with unknown person overlap explicitly acknowledged, but it would have a weaker novelty case. Do not manufacture replacement identities.

[SMHD (Cohan et al., 2018)](https://aclanthology.org/C18-1126/) is a plausible replacement or replication source: it includes self-reported anxiety diagnoses, matched observed controls, and removed mental-health-related posts. It is still self-report, not clinician-confirmed anxiety. Check current availability, data-use agreement, permitted research scope, provenance, timestamps, and redistribution rules before planning around it. The paper describes controlled access; access was not obtained in this audit. Depression, stress, or suicide datasets cannot be relabelled as anxiety to obtain an “external validation.”

Anxiety-specific clinical claims require appropriately licensed participant data with a validated anxiety measure such as GAD-7 and a justified outcome/threshold. A scale threshold is not the same as a clinician diagnosis. This is a separate, more expensive route with clinical collaboration, ethics determination, and enough independent participants. It is not required for a carefully limited computational-language paper.

| P2 | Coefficient/feature stability, collinearity and error audit | Document scales and feature families; assess stability across training/split seeds; examine false positives/negatives with permitted, non-identifying summaries |
| P2 | Real-user prefix comparison | Matched users across k and full history, observed chronology, retention and elapsed-time distributions. “Three posts” is not “one week,” and a prefix is not a preclinical prediction |

A transformer or fixed pretrained embedding baseline is useful, particularly if the contribution claims a competitive interpretable alternative. It is not necessary to fine-tune an LLM just to increase model count. First run TF-IDF properly; compare accuracy, calibration, CPU/runtime, and explanation tradeoffs. A simple model does not have to beat every complex one to be worth publishing.

Predefine a primary metric and contrast: user-level macro-F1, 13 linguistic features versus TF-IDF LR. Also report positive-class F1, precision/recall, ROC-AUC, average precision with prevalence, Brier score, log loss and calibration plots. Test-set thresholds must be frozen; select operating thresholds/calibration on separate training/development users if needed. Confidence intervals on paired differences matter more than a collection of unpaired significance claims. Correct prespecified multi-feature hypothesis families (e.g. BH FDR); treat additional model comparisons as exploratory or apply an appropriate correction. The new runner labels its exploratory contrasts as unadjusted.

Use multiple train/split seeds for stability without selecting the best test result. Keep the original test set as already observed evidence, not a newly untouched holdout. Seek a new temporal or external evaluation for the revised confirmatory question. Do not invent a retrospective preregistration: register the revision's prospective analysis plan and disclose previous exposure to the old test data.

## What current literature changes about the paper

These sources were retrieved from official ACL Anthology records and, for the central claims below, full PDFs. This is a targeted review, not an exhaustive systematic review.

- [Harrigian, Aguirre & Dredze, 2021](https://aclanthology.org/2021.clpsych-1.2/): data quality and availability are field-wide bottlenecks. A large post count does not overcome weak ground truth.
- [Ireland & Iserman, 2018](https://aclanthology.org/W18-0620/): dictionary and word analyses already distinguish anxiety support context from forum-member language in neutral communities, with held-out users. Our nearest-community, frozen later-period and resampled-training checks are an incremental extension, not the first context-sensitive anxiety-language study.
- [Harrigian & Dredze, 2022](https://aclanthology.org/2022.clpsych-1.6/): self-disclosures change in relevance over time; recommends diagnosis dates/comorbidity, matched controls, and removing selection artifacts. This directly motivates a stronger control design.
- [Burkhardt et al., 2022](https://aclanthology.org/2022.clpsych-1.9/): emotion and non-emotion linguistic features were already compared with symptom-scale data from over 6,500 therapy patients. Sentiment plus self-reference is not a new clinical insight by itself; abstract and official metadata examined.
- [Mitrović et al., 2024](https://aclanthology.org/2024.clpsych-1.12/): already studies panic versus anxiety language using lexical, emotion, attitude and transformer analyses on Reddit/Quora. Different constructs and source effects must be distinguished.
- [Shani & Stade, 2025](https://aclanthology.org/2025.clpsych-1.6/): argues for validated, dimensional, and transdiagnostic measurement. It directly challenges interpreting proxy classification as diagnosis-specific psychological evidence.
- [Marker et al., 2026](https://aclanthology.org/2026.clpsych-1.14/): directly examines person-level aggregation, document-tuned transformer representations and robustness. An author-level aggregation comparison needs a current baseline and a specific incremental question.
- [Cohan et al., 2018](https://aclanthology.org/C18-1126/), and [Yates, Cohan & Goharian, 2017](https://aclanthology.org/D17-1322/): established matched-control, person-level mental-health datasets and baselines. They preclude claiming author-disjoint evaluation or interpretable linguistic analysis as a newly established standard.
- [TRIPOD+AI, BMJ 2024](https://www.bmj.com/content/385/bmj-2023-078378): relevant reporting guidance if clinical prediction is retained; explicitly covers distinct evaluation participants, discrimination, calibration, fairness and transparency. Use it as a reporting aid, not as a certification that the present proxy study is a clinical model.
- [Official USC DAIC-WOZ documentation](https://dcapswoz.ict.usc.edu/): describes 189 sessions and academic/nonprofit access subject to consent constraints. The dataset is not unrestricted simply because it is downloadable to researchers.

Audit every bibliography entry, author list, DOI, dataset, result, and claimed limitation. In particular, delete the cross-dataset “our F1 is competitive” comparison unless it is accurately contextualized; it is not an experimental ranking.

### Verified submission considerations

JCSS [submission guidelines](https://link.springer.com/journal/42001/submission-guidelines) were read. They require a Data Availability Statement, clear disclosure of restrictions, appropriate ethics/consent statements, and human author accountability. They currently request LaTeX source plus a compiled PDF and recommend the Springer template's `iicol` option. Their AI policy requires disclosure of substantial generative/editorial use beyond basic copy editing. This assistance includes research and code generation; the human author should verify all work and follow that policy. A preprint does not supply peer review or institutional ethics approval.

The manuscript's self-declaration that the work is Not Human Subjects Research is not documentation of an institutional determination. Obtain the appropriate institutional determination, approval, or exemption as applicable; report who made it and the basis. Do not invent an IRB number. Public posts can be searchable and sensitive; release code, manifests and permitted derived artifacts, while complying with actual dataset terms and protecting identities. Do not infer protected demographics merely to manufacture a fairness table. If validated demographic data do not exist, disclose the limit.
