# Secondary-analysis statement worksheet for the human authors

This is a factual preparation document, not an ethics approval or institutional decision. Complete the missing author/institution information and have the appropriate body determine applicability under your local rules.

## Facts available for that determination

The project analyzes previously collected Reddit Mental Health Dataset (RMHD), [Kaggle version 1](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/entenam/reddit-mental-health-dataset). Its publisher lists CC0. The source study is Rani et al. 2024, DOI 10.3390/app14041547, which reports Victoria University HREC HRE23-005 (29 May 2023). The present secondary project is separate.

The revised research question concerns predictive differences between anxiety-community and other mental-health-community language. Community membership is the outcome, not diagnosis, treatment eligibility, clinical risk or onset. Available exports include sensitive text and observed account identifiers. There is no recruitment, contact, intervention or individual clinical decision in this analysis. No demographics are inferred and no identifiable examples, individual predictions or fitted vocabularies are included in the public research release.

Identifiers are used privately to prevent account overlap and deduplicate observations. Public artifacts are aggregate tables/figures, code, protocols and file hashes. Sensitive inputs and fitted text artifacts are retained separately from Git in access-restricted directories. Define your actual institution's storage access, retention/deletion period and handling policy before stating them in a journal submission.
